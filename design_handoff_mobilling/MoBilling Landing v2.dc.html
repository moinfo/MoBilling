HOSTING & DOMAINS

Everything WHMCS does — built in

Sell cPanel hosting and .tz domains with end-to-end automation: order, pay, provision, renew, suspend. No license fees, no plugins, no cron babysitting.

dns01

cPanel hosting automation

Connect your WHM server: accounts are created the moment an invoice is paid, suspended on non-payment, upgraded with prorated billing.

language02

.tz domain registrar

Register, renew and transfer .tz domains directly at the TCRA/tzNIC registry over EPP. Live availability search, your own pricing per TLD.

autorenew03

Auto-renewals from wallet

Clients opt in to auto-renew — renewals invoice themselves and draw from the client's credit balance.

lock04

SSL & DNS management

Nightly SSL monitoring on every domain, self-service nameserver changes and EPP transfer codes — all from the portal.

login05

One-click cPanel login

Clients jump into cPanel, webmail, file manager or phpMyAdmin from their portal — no passwords to share.

shopping_cart06

Order → pay → provision

A WHMCS-style cart: clients choose a plan, pick a domain, pay online — and the service goes live automatically.
